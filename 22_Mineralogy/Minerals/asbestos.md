- naturally occurring mineral substance that can be pulled into a *fluffy* consistency
- fibers are soft and flexible
- resistant to heat, electricity and chemical corrosion

## Industrial Uses
- fireproofing
- insulation
- brake lining
- asbestos cement

considered non-commercial due to carcinogenic property

- local asbestos was classified into:
	- shingles
	- paper
	- plaster
	- refuse grades
- market requirements used to depend mainly on fiber length
- in electrical insulation, iron content should not exceed 3.5%
