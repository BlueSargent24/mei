#mineral/group #nonmetallic 
## Industrial
- promotes fusion during firing and imparts strength, toughness, durability

1. production of glass, fired clay products, enamel paints
2. mild abrasives and scouring soaps
## deposits

1. Ilocos Norte
2. Nueva Ecija
3. Occidental Mindoro
4. Lanao del Norte


## Gemstones


| Plagioclase varieties |                                                                                 |

| **Labradorite**           | colorful, iridescent, also transparent stones in yellow, orange, red, and green |
| **Sunstone**              | gold spangles from inclusions of hematite                                       |
| **Peristerite**           | blue white iridescence                                                          |

| Alkali varieties |                                                                |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Orthoclase**       | pale yellow, flesh red                                         |
| **Amazonite**        | yellow green to greenish blue                                  |
| **moonstone**        | colorless, also white to yellowish, and reddish to bluish gray |
