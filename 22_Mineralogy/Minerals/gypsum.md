- hydrous calcium sulphate
- contains around 20% water
## Occurrence
- associated with [[anhydrite]] and in the more intensely altered volcanic rocks, with pyrite and some copper sulfides
- common [[Sulfates|sulfate]] precipitated from seawater
	- converts into [[anhydrite]] when it loses water (such as when it is deeply buried)
## Uses
1. cement retarder
2. fabrication of fireproof gypsum board
3. soil conditioner

## Varieties

- massive
### Satin spar
- fibrous

- crystalline
