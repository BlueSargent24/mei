#### Geothermometry
- study of **geothermometers**
	- mineral system containing miscibility gaps wherein we can use to mineral compositions to determine the temperature at which a rock equilibrated
- based on **thermodynamics**
	- at high temp, solid-solution minerals may have intermediate compositions, but at low temperatures they tend to unmix so that compositions are relatively close to end members

#### Fluid inclusions studies
- reliable method in measuring:
	- temperature of deposition in minerals
	- chemistry of the ore fluids including ssalinities

| Types           |                                                                                                  |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Primary         | inclusions formed in growing crystals, no leakage                                                |
| Secondary       | along fracture and sealed again, records conditions of later hydrothermal stage                  |
| Pseudosecondary | looks like Secondary but may not be if the host crystal was fractured as the crystal was growing |
#### Techniques employed
1. Direct measurement - the measurements of lavas, fumaroles, hot springs yields the maximum temperature of formation of minerals contained therein
2. Melting points - indicate the maximum temperatures at which they can crystallize from a melt; lower temperature in supersaturated solution and presence of other constituents
3. Inversion points - the transformation of some minerals' **crystal system** to another is **temperature dependent**
4. Exsolution - indicate temperature of formation that at which exsolution takes place
5. Recrystallization - similar to inversion and exsolution but applies more specifically to native metals
6. Fluid inclusions - indicate the approximate temperature of formation of the crystals by the amount of contraction of the liquid, assuming that the liquid originally filled the cavity
7. Stable [[Stable isotopes|isotope]] - indirect method
