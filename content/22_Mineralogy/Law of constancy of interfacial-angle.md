>[!note] law of constancy of interfacial angles 
>measured at the same temperature, similar angles on crystals of the same substance remain constant, regardless the size and the shape of the crystals
^lawconstancyangle

![[angle constancy.png]]

In [[Crystallography]], the interfacial angle to a crystal is the angle subtended between the normal drawn on the two faces.

![[interfacial angle.png]]

- interfacial angles of crystals of a *particular* mineral -> always constant
