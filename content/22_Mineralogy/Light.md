- a form of energy
- detectable with the eye
- transmitted from one place to another at finite velocity
- portion of the **electromagnetic spectrum**
	- radiation from cosmic rays to radio waves


## How light behaves
**Particle theory**
- energy from a photon when an atom is excited
**Wave theory**
- light travels as a wave

**Electromagnetic radiation theory of light**
- electric and magnetic components vibrate at right angles to the direction of propagation
- *electric vector* - electric component referred to as the vibration direction of the light ray

**Dispersion** - separation of visible light into its different colors
**Wave** - sinusoidal movement
**Ray** - rectilinear path followed by the wave

| Colors | Wavelengths in µm |
| ------ | ----------------- |
| Violet | 410               |
| Blue   | 480               |
| Green  | 530               |
| Yellow | 580               |
| Orange | 630               |
| Red    | 710               |
For all isotropic media, the direction of vibration and of propagation are perpendicular


# Light wave
- the velocity and wavelength are related in the ff eqn:
$$F=\frac{V}{\lambda}$$
F = frequency in cycles/second or Hertz (Hz)
In optical mineralogy, F = constant

**Wave front** - parallel surface connecting similar or equivalent points on adjacent waves

**Wave normal** - line perpendicular to the wave front; direction of the wave movement

**Light ray** - direction of propagation of the light energy


## Phase and Retardation
Retardation - δ in nanometers; distance that one ray lags behind another
- if whole number - the two waves are **in phase** ; R = A+B
- if 1/2, 1 1/2, and so on, the waves are **out of phase** ; R has zero amplitude if A and B are equal but opposite

## Law of reflection
Angle of incidence - between incident and normal
Angle of reflection - between reflected ray and normal

### Reflection and Refraction

Refraction - light is bent when passing from one material to another at an angle

Index of Refraction (n) - measure of how effective a material is in bending light
*Most minerals have n values 1.4-2.0*

**Snell's Law**
$$\frac{\sin{\theta}_{air}}{\sin{\theta}_{watee}}=\frac{n_{water}}{n_{air}}$$
used to calculate how much the light will bend on travelling into the new medium
