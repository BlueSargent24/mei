# Garnet
- more complex than olivines
- general formula is **A₃²⁺B₂³⁺(SiO₄)₃** where:
	- A is Ca²⁺, Mg²⁺, Fe²⁺, Mn²⁺
	- B is Al³⁺, Fe³⁺, Cr³⁺
