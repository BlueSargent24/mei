## Industrial Use
- produced and sold in several forms
	- jig concentrate
	- crude lumps
	- ground barite
	- flotation concentrate

| Use                            | Preparation                                                      |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| oil well drilling              | ground to -325 mesh                                              |
| production of barium compounds | sold as jig concentrate                                          |
| glass industry                 | prepared to -20 mesh & freed from iron using magnetic separators |
| inert filler                   | ground to -325 mesh                                              |
| pigment or extenders           | bleached with acid treatment to remove iron stain                |
## Deposits
- Ph production started in January 1973 but ceased in 1991
