### Aluminosilicate Group
- 6-fold sites in kyanite, 5-fold sites in andalusite and 4-fold sites in sillimanite
- Kyanite is 14% denser than andalusite and 11.5% denser than sillimanite
	- kyanite to be stable at the highest pressure and lowest temperatures
- Andalusite is the low pressure phase
- Sillimanite is stable at high temperatures and moderate pressure
