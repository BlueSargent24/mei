# olivine
- refers to the Mg₂SiO₄ - Fe₂SiO₄ series (solid-solution), Mg and Fe can substitute for each other
	- relative size of cations control the solid solution formation
- isolated tetrahedra structure
- at higher temperatures, the structure expands by increasing the size of the M sites, and so more solid solution is possible
	- At 1000°C, there is about 5% substitution of Ca for Fe, Mg
	- At 1450°C, it is 20%

## Occurrence

![[Diagram Seismic and density structures of the lower mantle showing the major discontinuities.png]]

At the high temperatures and pressures found at depth within the Earth the olivine structure is no longer stable. Below depths of about 410 km olivine transforms into [[wadsleyite]] and, at about 520 km depth, wadsleyite transforms into [[ringwoodite]], which has the [[spinel]] structure. At a depth of about 660 km, ringwoodite decomposes into silicate [[perovskite]] known as [[bridgmanite]] (Mg,Fe)SiO3 and [[ferropericlase]] (Mg,Fe)O. These phase transitions lead to a discontinuous increase in the density of the Earth's mantle that can be observed by seismic methods
