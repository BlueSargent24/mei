## Spinel Group (Isometric)
- both tetrahedral and octahedral sites are occupied by metal ions
- "normal": each metal species is found in either tetrahedral or octahedral coordination but not both
- one metal species occupies both coordinations
### Inverse Spinels
- [[spinel]] (MgAl₂O₄)
- [[magnetite]] (Fe₃O₄)

### Normal Spinels
