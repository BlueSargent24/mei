## Occurrence

Geological setting:
Chrysotile forms through the hydrothermal alteration of ultramafic rocks such as peridotite, dunite, and serpentinite. It commonly develops in veins and slip-fiber fillings within serpentinized rocks.

Associated minerals:
Often found with [[antigorite]], [[lizardite]], [[magnetite]], [[brucite]], and [[chromite]].

Major deposits:
Significant sources include Canada (Quebec, Ontario), Russia (Ural Mountains), Zimbabwe, and Italy.
