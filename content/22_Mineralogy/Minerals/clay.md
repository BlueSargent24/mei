## Types
### Residual clays
- in situ clays formed by weathering due to chemical and hydrothermal process (e.g kaolin)
- biggest reserves of **siliceous clay**: ***Siruma, Camarines Sur*** formed by the residual alteration of chloritic schists
- alteration of basalt or andesite by hot sulfuric water from solfataras or fumaroles

### Transported
- accumulation of clayey materials in sites such as swamps and basins (e.g ball clay, fire clay)
- consist of sedimentary beds associated w/ peat or lignite, alluvial and floodplain clays
- **Fire clays**: clay beds underlying peat or lignite
	- **Uneng, Semirara Island**
- **Alluvial and floodplain clays**: buff or red burning clays
	- under rice paddies and flat areas
	- high iron oxide content
