## Occurrence

- generated deep in the mantle, in the layer known as Transition zone between the lower and upper mantle at around 400-650 km depth

### geological setting
Deep mantle-derived rocks such as [[Kimberlites]], [[lamprophyre]], and others. With decreasing pressure, the diamonds dissolve back into the rock. To occur and survive in a metastable state at the surface they must arrive from depth quickly and very often crystals show dissolution features because the transport to the surface is not quick enough.
Since diamond is extremely hard, it survives in alluvia.
