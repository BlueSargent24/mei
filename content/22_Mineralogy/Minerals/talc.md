## Occurrence

- Schists and steatite through hydrothermal alteration of mafic rocks. Low-temperature metamorphism of siliceous dolomites.

## Industrial
- produce steatite or soapstone
- Essential properties
	- whiteness
	- softness and smoothness
	- lubricating power
	- chemical inertness
	- high fusion point
	- low electrical conductivity
	- high absorption of certain types of greases and oils
