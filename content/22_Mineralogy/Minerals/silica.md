#mineral/group #nonmetallic 

## Occurrence
- mineral quartz:
	- veins and lenses of bull quartz
	- sand, pebbles, cobble and boulders
	- siliceous clays
## Deposits

| Silica Deposit     | Occurrence                                      |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Negros Occidental  | thermal springs and vapors related to volcanism |
| Lubang and Palawan | quartz sand                                     |
| Quezon province    | bull quartz as pegmatite dikes / small lenses   |
## Industrial
1. Silica sand: manufacture of glass containers, sheet glass, ferrosilicon, sodium silicate, cleanser, abrasive, additive etc.
2. rushed and pulverized bull quartz: sanitary wares and flint glass containers

Others:
- [[gemstones|gemstone]]
- flux
- filter
- essential component of electronics

## Siliceous clays
- deposits of siliceous white clay in chlorite schists derived from igneous rocks (Camarines Sur)
- hydrothermal alteration in schists
