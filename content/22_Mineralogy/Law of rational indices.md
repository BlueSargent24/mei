# Miller indices
- ratio of crystal axes
- uses the relative intercepts of the faces with the axes
- reciprocals of parameters

characteristics:
1. in all crystal systems (except hexagonal), they are three digit (hkl)

steps:
1. determining the parameters
2. inverting the parameters
3. clearing the fractions
