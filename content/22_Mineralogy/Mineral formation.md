# Environmental processes @Earthmaterials

| Process                             |                                                                                                      |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Precipitation from solution         | surface water, groundwater, hydrothermal solutions                                                   |
| Sublimation from a gas              | occurs where volcanic gases are vented; or where gas phases separate from solution in the subsurface |
| Crystallization from a melt/liquid  | [[Magma]]                                                                                            |
| Solid state growth                  | new mineral crystal grow from the constituents of pre-existing minerals                              |
| Solid-liquid or solid-gas reactions | common in weathering through vein formation to metamorphism                                          |
