### Normal / Barite Type
- Orthorhombic Bipyramidal or Holohedral
### Hemimorphic / Calamine Type
- Orthorhombic Pyramidal
### Sphenoidal / Epsomite Type
- Orthorhombic Bisphenoidal

# Orthorhombic Minerals
