| Solution / Reagent                          | Main Use                     | Best Minerals / Elements Distinguished      | Principle / Reaction                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Alizarin Red S**                          | Carbonate stain              | Calcite vs Dolomite                         | Calcite stains red rapidly in weak acid solution; dolomite reacts weakly or slowly. Common carbonate petrography stain. |
| **Trypan blue**                             | Biological / pore stain      | Porosity, organic material                  | Organic dye absorbed by dead cells or porous material; not a standard mineral ID stain.                                 |
| **Silver chromate**                         | Halide identification        | Halite and chloride-bearing phases          | Formation of reddish-brown Ag₂CrO₄ precipitate in chloride reactions.                                                   |
| **Copper nitrate**                          | Feldspar staining            | Potassium feldspar vs Plagioclase           | Used with sodium cobaltinitrite; K-feldspar becomes yellow due to potassium reaction.                                   |
| **Cobalt nitrate**                          | Flame/mineral test           | Aluminum-bearing minerals                   | Heating produces characteristic blue coloration with alumina-bearing minerals.                                          |
| **Harris Hematoxylin**                      | Clay/organic stain           | Clay minerals, organic matter               | Histological dye adapted for fine-grained mineral or organic staining.                                                  |
| **Meigen reagent**                          | Carbonate differentiation    | Aragonite vs Calcite                        | Aragonite turns black after boiling due to silver precipitation; calcite remains unstained.                             |
| **Rhodizonic Acid**                         | Barium/lead test             | Barite and Pb minerals                      | Produces colored complexes with Ba²⁺ or Pb²⁺ ions.                                                                      |
| **Tropaeolin**                              | Alkali mineral stain         | Potassium-bearing minerals                  | Organic azo dye sensitive to alkali reactions.                                                                          |
| **Hydrogen Peroxide – Potassium Hydroxide** | Manganese detection          | Pyrolusite and Mn-bearing minerals          | Oxidation forms colored manganate compounds in alkaline solution.                                                       |
| **Lemberg hydroxide**                       | Dolomite identification      | Dolomite vs Calcite                         | Detects Mg in dolomite through characteristic reaction/color behavior.                                                  |
| **Benzidine**                               | Sulfide/sulfate indication   | Sulfur-bearing minerals                     | Oxidation reaction gives blue coloration; historically used for sulfides.                                               |
| **Potassium ferricyanide**                  | Iron detection               | Ferroan vs non-ferroan carbonates/silicates | Ferric-ferrocyanide complexes form blue coloration with Fe²⁺.                                                           |
| **Organic Dye Titan Yellow**                | Magnesium test               | Mg-bearing minerals like Dolomite           | Forms red/orange complex with magnesium ions.                                                                           |
| **Organic Dye Eosin Y**                     | Carbonate/organic staining   | Calcite and fossil structures               | Pink-red dye selectively absorbed by certain carbonate phases.                                                          |
| **Organic Dye Congo Red**                   | Clay/organic staining        | Clay minerals, cellulose-like materials     | Adsorption dye; also used in histology.                                                                                 |
| **Organic Dye Alizarin Red**                | Carbonate staining           | Calcite vs Dolomite                         | Similar to Alizarin Red S; calcite stains strongly red.                                                                 |
| **Feigl reagent**                           | Manganese/mineral spot tests | Mn-bearing minerals                         | Produces diagnostic color reactions from oxidation-reduction chemistry.                                                 |
| **Sodium cobaltinitrite**                   | Potassium detection          | Potassium feldspar                          | Potassium forms yellow sodium potassium cobaltinitrite precipitate.                                                     |
| **Kirchberg reagent**                       | Feldspar differentiation     | K-feldspar vs plagioclase                   | Selective staining in feldspar identification.                                                                          |
| **Magneson**                                | Magnesium detection          | Dolomite and Mg-rich silicates              | Forms blue complex with Mg under alkaline conditions.                                                                   |
