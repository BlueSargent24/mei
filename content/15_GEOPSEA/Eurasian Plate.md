# Eurasian Plate
- Almost synonymous with the **Sunda Plate**
-  continental except for marginal basins created along the edges
- weak plate movement with respect to hot spots
- **50 Ma** - fixed orientation
## Sunda Plate
- region west of the Philippine arc, "Sundaland Block"
- Late Miocene - Sunda Plate decoupled from Eurasian Plate

## Proto-South China Sea
- Rifting initiated ~50 Ma, leading to the development of marginal basins.  
- Parts of the Philippine mobile belt may have originated from the northern margins of this basin.
# Basins

## South China Sea Basin

- *Spreading center*: Scarborough Ridge
- *Subduction zone*: Manila Trench
- *Sub-basins*: Northwest, Southwest, and Central subbasins
- *Spreading age*: Oligocene
