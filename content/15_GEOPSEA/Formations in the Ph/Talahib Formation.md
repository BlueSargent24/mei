  

The Talahib Formation of Ocampo (1971) is equivalent to the Caguray Formation in Mindoro. Ocampo (1971) measured a thickness of 2,046 m for the exposures along Tumalo River and its tributaries - Panaraon and Talahib creeks - for the Talahib Formation.
