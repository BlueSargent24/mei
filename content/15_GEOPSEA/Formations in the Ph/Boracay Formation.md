  

The Boracay Formation was named by MMAJ-JICA (1986) for the exposures of clastic rocks with intercalated tuff and basaltic flow breccia in Masbate. It was presumed to be Late Jurassic in age (MMAJ-JICA, 1990). The Boracay was renamed Calumpang Formation by MGB (2004) for the exposures near Bgy. Calumpang in Masbate.
