

---

Lithology: Marl

Distribution: Omanay, North Cotabato

Age: Pleistocene

Thickness: 35 m

Named_by: MGB (2004)

---

 

The Omanay Marl was named after Omanay in northern North Cotabato. The marl is greenish cream with abundant small foraminifera and contains perfectly preserved oyster beds. It was dated Pleistocene and has a thickness of 35 m.
