Distribution: Caracaran River, Batan Island

Age: Early Miocene

Previous_name: Caracaran Silt (Corby and others, 1951)

Renamed_by: MGB (2004)

This formation was named Caracaran Silt by Corby and others (1951) for the fine grained clastic rocks along Caracaran River, Batan Island. It consists of thin bedded lignite-bearing siltstone with lenticular limestone interbeds and coal beds. The Caracaran was dated Early Miocene based on the presence of _Lepidocylina_, _Miogypsina_ and _Operculinella_ in the limestone. The thickness is about 90 m. It grades into the coal measures of the Liguan Formation and could represent a facies of the latter.
