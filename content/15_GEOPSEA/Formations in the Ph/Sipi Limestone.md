 

The Sipi Limestone was named by Capistrano (1951) for the exposures of Late Eocene limestone along Sipi creek, a tributary of Bato River in Catanduanes Island. The massive light to dark gray limestone is fossiliferous and estimated to be 250 m thick. The Sipi could be partly equivalent to the Hilawan Limestone facies of the Payo Formation.
