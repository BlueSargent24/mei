  

The San Mateo Clastics consists predominantly of tuffaceous sandstone and pebble conglomerate. It interfingers with or lie conformably above the Awang-Table Limestone. These two units represent the equivalent in the north of the Pliocene Marbel Formation that occupies the southern part of the Cotabato Basin. (_see [Marbel Formation](Marbel%20Formation.md)_)
