  

The Balinsasayao Formation of Ayson (1987) apparently corresponds to the Pleistocene eruptive products of Cuernos de Negros, which is part of the Canlaon Volcanic Complex. The pile of andesite flows and pyroclastic rocks comprising the Balinsasayao are estimated to total at least 950 m thick (Tebar, 1984 in Ayson, 1987).
