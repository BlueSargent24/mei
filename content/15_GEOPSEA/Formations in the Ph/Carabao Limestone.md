  

The Carabao Limestone was named by Vallesteros and Argaño (1965) for the Permian limestone exposure on Carabao Island. This was renamed Pacul Limestone by MGB (2004) to avoid repletion of the geographic name carried by Carabao Sandstone in accordance with the Philippine Stratigraphic Guide. (_see [Pacul Limestone](Pacul%20Limestone.md)_).
