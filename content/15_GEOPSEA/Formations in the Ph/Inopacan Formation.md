This unit was originally named [Inopacan Clastics](Inopacan%20Clastics.md) by Florendo (1987), here renamed Inopacan Formation. It consists mainly of well-sorted rounded pebble- and cobble conglomerate with minor poorly bedded mudstone and calcareous tuff. It is exposed in the northern, western and southern parts of Maasin. The Inopacan unconformably overlies the Pangasugan Formation and is apparently equivalent to the [Dolores Formation](Dolores%20Formation.md), both of which are grouped under Leyte Central Highlands. It is dated Late Pliocene on the basis of its stratigraphic position and similarity with the Dolores Formation.
