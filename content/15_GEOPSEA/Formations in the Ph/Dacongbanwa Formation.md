  

The term Dacongbanwa Formation was used by the MMAJ-JICA (1973) to refer to the massive Middle Miocene coralline limestone at the northwestern slope of Mount Agtuuganon. A review of its description shows that the Dacongbanwa is synonymous to the Agtuuganon Limestone. (_see [Agtuuganon Limestone](Agtuuganon%20Limestone.md)_)
