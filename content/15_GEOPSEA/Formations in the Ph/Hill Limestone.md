  

The Hill Limestone is a member of the Liguan Formation and consists of massive gray to white limestone forming cliffs from north Liguan Point to the area north of Caracaran. _Miogypsina_, _Lepidocyclina_ (_Nephrolepidina_ and _Trybliolepidina_) and _Operculina_ characterize the fossil assemblages of this unit. It is about 350 m thick_. (see [Liguan Formation](Liguan%20Formation.md)_)
