  

The King Ranch Formation was named by MGB (1984) for the sandstone-shale sequence at King Ranch in Busuanga, northern Palawan. The sandstone is thickly bedded and arkosic to quartzose in composition. The shale is gray to black and ranges from silty shale to muddy shale. The King Ranch is partly equivalent to the Coron Formation. (_see [Coron Formation](Coron%20Formation.md)_)
