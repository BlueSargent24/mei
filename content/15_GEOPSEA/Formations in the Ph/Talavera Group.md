  

The Talavera Group was introduced by Huth (1962) for the exposures cropping out in the hills bordering western Cebu east of Talavera Bay and the municipality of Talavera located in central Cebu. These consist of [Toledo](Toledo%20Formation.md) and [Maingit](Maingit%20Formation.md) formations earlier described by Corby and others (1951).
