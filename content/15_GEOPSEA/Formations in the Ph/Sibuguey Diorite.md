  

The Sibuguey Diorite was named by Antonio (1972) for the diorite and quartz diorite stocks in Sibuguey Peninsula, Mindanao. It was renamed Midsalip Diorite by MGB (2004) for the diorite bodies in Midsalip and other areas in east-central Zamboanga in deference to the priority for the name Sibuguey Formation of Brown (1950_).
