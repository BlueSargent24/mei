  

The Salvacion Limestone of Culp and Madrid (1967) is equivalent to the [Pilar Limestone](Pilar%20Limestone.md). It is the oldest sedimentary unit in Guimaras Island. The limestone occurs in Barrio Salvacion and Panobolon Island south of Guimaras. It was dated Late Oligocene on the basis of orbitoidal foraminifera and has an estimated thickness of 700 m (Culp and Madrid, 1967).
