  

The Upper Zigzag Formation of BED (1986a) and Caagusan (1978) for the sequence in Cagayan Valley that spans the age range of Late Oligocene to Early Miocene may be considered equivalent to the [Lubuagan Formation](Lubuagan%20Formation.md). However, BED (1986a) and Caagusan (1978) regards this sequence of clastic rocks as coeval with the [Ibulao Limestone](Ibulao%20Limestone.md).
