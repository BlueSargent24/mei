  

The Tacloban Volcanics was named by Pilac (1965) for the pillow basalt in Tacloban area. It was renamed Caibaan Pillow Basalt by Balce and Cabantog (1998) and as Caibaan Basalt by MGB (2004). The pillow basalt represents the volcanic carapace of the Tacloban Ophiolite. (_see [Caibaan Basalt](Caibaan%20Basalt.md)_)
