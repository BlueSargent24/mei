  

The Lower Zigzag Formation was named by Caagusan (1978) for the Oligocene red and green clastic beds in Cagayan Valley. It may be correlated with the Dibuluan Formation. The estimated thickness of the Lower Zigzag Formation is 1,800 m (BED, 1986a). (_see [Dibuluan Formation](Dibuluan%20Formation.md)_)
