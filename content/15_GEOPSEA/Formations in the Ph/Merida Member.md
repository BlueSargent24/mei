  

The Merida Member and Tinobdan Limestone of the Hubay Formation in western Leyte were originally recognized by Maac-Aguilar (1995) as formations. These are probably facies of the Hubay, representing differing proportions of the limestone and clastic contents of the units. The Tinobdan Limestone is probably the shallow water counterpart of the calcareous conglomerate, sandstone and shales of the Merida and contemporaneous deposition is postulated for the limestone and the calcareous clastics. The Early Pliocene light gray to white bentonitic marls and marly siltstones sampled by Porth and others (1989) in a tributary of the Salug River, near Barrio Kapodlusan, east of Hilongos, west-central Leyte is considered part of the Merida.
