

The Ananawin Formation of PNOC (1979, cited in BED, 1986c) is considered equivalent to the Bugtong Formation in Mindoro. It has an estimated gross thickness of 300 m with an age of Late Oligocene to Middle Miocene as determined from paleontological dating (BED, 1986c). (_see [Bugtong Formation](Bugtong%20Formation.md)_)
