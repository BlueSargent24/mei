  

The Masonson Schist was named by Capistrano and Magpantay (1958) for the foliated rocks in eastern Panay. The Masonson and the Sibala Formation was presumed by BMG (1981) to constitute the basement of Eastern Panay and assigned a Cretaceoous-Paleogene age. Some of the exposures of the schist could be localized effects of shearing in the Sibala Formation.
