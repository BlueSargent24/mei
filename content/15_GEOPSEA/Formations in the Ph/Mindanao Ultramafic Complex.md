  

The Mindanao Ultramafic Complex was previously named by Antonio (1972) for the exposures of serpentinized peridotites, dunite and pyroxenite between Sindangan and Molave in north-central Zamboanga. Later the ultramafic rocks were recognized as components of an ophiolite complex designated by Yumul and others (2000) as Polanco Ophiolite. (_see [Polanco Ophiolite](Polanco%20Ophiolite.md)_)
