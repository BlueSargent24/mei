  

The San Juan Limestone was named by Corby and others (1951) for the white porous limestone exposure in Bgy. San Juan, Ponson Island, one of the islands of the Camotes Island Group. It also occupies large areas on Poro and Pacijan islands, and comprises the most widespread formation in the Camotes Islands. The limestone has a thickness of about 200 m and is believed to be Pliocene in age.
