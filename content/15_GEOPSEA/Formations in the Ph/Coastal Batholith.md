  

Huge bodies of diorite, tonalite and gabbro on the eastern side of the Northern Sierra Madre Range of Luzon were collectively designated by MMAJ-JICA (1977) as Coastal Batholith. It was renamed Dinalungan Diorite Complex by MGB (2005). Radiometric K-Ar datings indicate a Middle Eocene age for the intrusive bodies. _(see [Dinalungan Diorite Complex](%20Dinalungan%20Diorite%20Complex.md)_ )
