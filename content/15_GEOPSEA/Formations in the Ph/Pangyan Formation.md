

---

Lithology: Sandstone, shale

Stratigraphic_relations: Conformable over Latian Limestone; overlain by Glan Formation

Distribution: Upper Big Lun River; Barrio Lawa; Margus and Pangyan rivers.

Age: late Early Miocene – early Middle Miocene

Thickness: ~ 300 m

Previous_Name: Nakal Formation (Froehlich and Melendres, 1960)

Named_by: MGB (2004)

---

 

This formation was previously considered by BED (1986b) to be identical to the Nakal Formation of Froehlich and Melendres (1960) west of Cotabato Valley. It was renamed Pangyan Formation by MGB (2004) for exposures along Pangyan River. The formation lies conformably over the Latian Limestone and is conformably overlain by the Glan Formation. The Pangyan consists of interbedded arkosic sandstone and calcareous thinly-bedded shale with intercalations of siltstone. Aside from Pangyan River, the formation also crops out at Upper Big Lun and Margus rivers and Barrio Lawa. Based on stratigraphic position, the Pangyan is assigned a late Early Miocene to early Middle Miocene age. It has an estimated thickness of 300 m along the Big Lun River (BED, 1986b).
