  

The Gutusan Member is the upper member of the Tuktuk Formation in western Leyte. It consists of thin bedded sandstone, limestone and shale outcropping along roadcuts in Gutusan. The thickness of the Gutusan near the Tuktuk type section is 250 m (Corby and others, 1951). (_see [Tuktuk Formation](Tuktuk%20Formation.md)_)
