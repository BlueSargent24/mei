Lithology: Volcanic and volcaniclastic rocks

Stratigraphic_relations: Capped by limestone.

Distribution: Malita River, Saranggani Peninsula

Age: Early Miocene?

Named_by: MGB (2004)

---

 

Occasional windows through Late Miocene volcanic rocks along Malita River expose slightly metamorphosed volcanic and volcaniclastic sequence capped by limestone (Pubellier and others, 1990). This unit is poorly described and is best known through core logs recovered from drill holes by the Philippine National Oil Company (PNOC). The limestone capping has been dated as Early to lowermost Middle Miocene (Langhian) based on its foraminiferal content (Pubellier and others, 1991). The unit is probably Early Miocene.
