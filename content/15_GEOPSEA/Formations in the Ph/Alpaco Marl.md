  

The term Alpaco Marl was used by Smith (1924) after its type locality at Barrio Alpaco, Naga, Cebu. It represents the upper member of the Malubog Formation and further subdivided into lower Binabac Limestone, lower coal measure, upper Binabac Limestone and upper coal measure. (_see [Malubog Formation](Malubog%20Formation.md)_).
