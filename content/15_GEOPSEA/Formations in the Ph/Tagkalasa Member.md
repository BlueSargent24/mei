  

The Tagkalasa Member is part of the Balabac Formation in Balabac Island. It is composed generally of arkosic, massive, light gray, moderately hard and fine to medium grained sandstone with thin layers of shale. The presence of several species of _Spiroclypeus_ and _Lepidocyclina_ in the member indicates that it was deposited during Early Miocene. Its thickness ranges from 500 to 800 m.
