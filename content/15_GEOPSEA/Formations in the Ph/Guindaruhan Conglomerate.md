  

The Guindaruhan Conglomerate of Balce (1974, in Hashimoto, 1977) is equivalent to the basal portion of the coal measures that represent the lower member of the Cebu Formation in central Cebu. The basal conglomerate of the coal measures at Guindaruhan is 10-15 m thick and contains clasts of volcanic rock, pyroclastic rocks and chert. (_see [Cebu Formation](Cebu%20Formation.md)_).
