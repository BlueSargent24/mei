  

The Lamon Andesite was named by MMAJ-JICA (1990) for the Middle Miocene andesite unconformably overlying the Late Oligocene Sambulawan Formation of UNDP (1984) along Lamon, Tugbo and Sambulawan Rivers in Masbate. The sedimentary sequence constituting the Sambulawan Formation is equivalent to the Nabangig Formation. (_see [Nabangig Formation](Nabangig%20Formation.md)_)
