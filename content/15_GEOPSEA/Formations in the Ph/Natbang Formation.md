  

The Natbang Formation of MMAJ-JICA (1977) exposed at Natbang, west of Bayombong, Nueva Vizcaya is probably equivalent to the [Palali Formation](Palali%20Formation.md). It is mainly made up of conglomerates with alternating beds of sandstones and mudstones and intercalated thin layers of basaltic lavas. It was dated Early to Middle Miocene.
