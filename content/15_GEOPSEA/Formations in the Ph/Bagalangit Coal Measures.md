

The Bagalangit Coal Measures was named by Corby and others (1951) for the exposures of Late Miocene siltstone with subordinate claystone and sandstone near the southern end of Burias Island. Beds exposed on the sea cliffs attain a thickness of 160m. The Bagalangit is probably a facies of the San Pascual Formation. (_see [San Pascual Formation](San%20Pascual%20Formation.md)_)
