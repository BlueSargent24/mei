  

The Tinobdan Limestone, together with the [Merida Member](Merida%20Member.md), is probably a facies of the [Hubay Formation](Hubay%20Limestone.md) in western Leyte. These were originally recognized by Maac-Aguilar (1995) as formations. As facies of the Hubay, these units show differing proportions of limestone and clastic contents. The Tinobdan Limestone is probably the shallow water counterpart of the calcareous conglomerate, sandstone and shales of the Merida and contemporaneous deposition is postulated for the limestone and the calcareous clastics. The Early Pliocene light gray to white bentonitic marls and marly siltstones sampled by Porth and others (1989) in a tributary of the Salug River, near Barrio Kapodlusan, east of Hilongos, west-central Leyte is considered part of the Merida.
