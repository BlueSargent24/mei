---

Lithology: Limestone

Stratigraphic_relations: Not reported

Distribution: Central part to southeastern tip of Cagraray Island

Age: Late Oligocene – Early Miocene

Thickness: < 100 m

Named_by: Corby and others (1951)

---

The Coal Harbor Lmestone was named by Corby and others (1951) for the limestone exposed from the central part of Cagraray Island to the southeastern tip at Cagraray Point. It is massive pink to buff limestone with a thickness of less than 100 m. Hashimoto and others (1981) recognized _Spiroclypeus_-rich and _Eulipidina-Miogypsina-Flosculinella_ assemblages for which a Late Oligocene to Early Miocene age was given to the unit.
