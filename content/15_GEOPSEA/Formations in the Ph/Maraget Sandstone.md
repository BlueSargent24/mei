  

The Maraget Sandstone represents the middle of three members of the Cabatuan Formation in Iloilo. Its type locality is at Barrio Maraget in Cabatuan, Iloilo. It also crops out as far as Calinog in the north and San Miguel in the south. The lower beds are principally siltstone with occasional coarse grained sandstone and mudstone layers. Cross-bedded, ferruginous, loosely consolidated, porous, light and permeable sandstone with white tuffaceous clay partings make up the uppermost beds. In some localities, lenses of conglomerate have been encountered. The sandstones are largely cross-bedded and contain megafossils, but no microfossils. The thickness varies but west of Calinog, it is about 150 m, while Santos (1968) measured a thickness of 352 m along the Duyanduyan-Maasin road section.
