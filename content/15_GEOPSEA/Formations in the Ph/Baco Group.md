  

The Baco Group of MMAJ-JICA (1984) is a suite of rocks exposed from Mamburao to Mansalay in Mindoro. It includes the Mansalay and Lumintao formations. The Group has a total thickness of around 5,000 m. (_see [Mansalay Formation](Mansalay%20Formation.md) and [Lumintao Basalt](Lumintao%20Basalt.md)_)
