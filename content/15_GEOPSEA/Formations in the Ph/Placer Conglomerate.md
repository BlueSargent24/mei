---

Lithology: Conglomerate with lenses of sandstone and mudstone

Distribution: Coastal area around Surigao del Norte and neighboring islands 

Age: Pleistocene

Thickness: 100 m

Named_by: Santos-Yñigo (1944)

---

 

Santos-Yñigo (1944) named the conglomerate with lenses of shale and sandstone in Placer, Surigao del Norte as Placer Conglomerate. Clasts of the conglomerate range from angular to sub-angular and granule to boulder sizes. Sorting is poor and bedding becomes apparent only where there are lenses of sandstone and shale. The formation is of Pleistocene age and is 100 m thick.
