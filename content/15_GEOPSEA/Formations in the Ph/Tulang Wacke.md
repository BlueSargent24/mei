  

The Tulang Wacke in Bohol is one of five members of the Ubay Formation that was subdivided by UNDP (1987). The Tulang outcrops southeast of Jetafe and was described as westward dipping beds of sandstone and siltstone apparently overlying another member, the Rizal Basaltic Wackes.
