---

Lithology: Chert; minor mudstone, shale, sandstone and vitric tuff

Stratigraphic_relations: Caps the Caibaan Basalt

Distribution: Barangay Palanog, between Tacloban and Palo areas, Leyte

Age: Cretaceous

Previous_name: Palanog Pelagic Sediments (Cabantog and Escalada, 1989)

Renamed_by: MGB (2004)

---

 

The pelagic sedimentary rocks capping the Caibaan Basalt were collectively designated by Cabantog and Escalada (1989) as Palanog Pelagic Sediments, renamed by MGB (2004) as Palanog Formation. The unit consists essentially of chert with subordinate mudstone, shale, sandstone and vitric tuff. Outcrops of these rocks are usually observed as irregular masses or patches in Tacloban and Palo area especially in Barangay Palanog. The chert contains microcrystalline quartz, hematite and recrystallized radiolarian tests. Microcrystalline calcite, smectite, zeolite and illite are distinctly discernible in the vitric tuff. The formation constitutes the pelagic sedimentary cover of the Tacloban Ophiolite.
