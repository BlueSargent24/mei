

---

Lithology: Limestone, conglomerate

Stratigraphic_relations: Not reported

Distribution: Oreng Hill and other hills in the southern part of Bongabon delta; Pula and Subaan-Singalan rivers, Oriental Mindoro

Age: Pleistocene

Named_by: Teves (1953)

Synonymy: Socorro Group (MMAJ-JICA, 1984)

---

 

The Oreng Formation was named by Teves (1953) for the limestone and conglomerate exposed as hills (Oreng, Mamilpil and others) bordering the southern part of the Bongabon delta in southeastern Oriental Mindoro. The limestone, which occupies the lower portion of the formation, is milky white, vuggy, sugary and fossiliferous. The overlying conglomerate consists of loosely cemented pebbles. The reef limestone that comprises Ilin and Ambulong islands and the area between Magsaysay and Bulalacao may be considered part of this formation. This is equivalent to the limestone of the Socorro Group of MMAJ-JICA (1984). The Oreng is also encountered along Pula River and Subaan-Singalan River in northeastern Oriental Mindoro (Agadier and Maac, 1987). Based on faunal association and stratigraphic relationships, Agadier and Maac (1987) assigned a Pleistocene, most probably Late Pleistocene, age for these rocks.
