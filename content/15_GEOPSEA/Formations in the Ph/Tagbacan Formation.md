---

Lithology: conglomerate, sandstone, shale

Stratigraphic_relations: Not reported

Distribution: Tagbacan Creek, Bukidnon

Age: Middle Miocene – Late Miocene

Named_by: Santiago (1983)

---

 

Well-bedded conglomerate, pebbly sandstone and fine tuffaceous sandstone and shale exposed along Tagbacan Creek were designated by Santiago (1983) as Tagbacan Formation. These rocks generally exhibit a light gray color that tarnishes to a brownish tint upon oxidation. A thrust fault defines the contact between this formation and ultramafic rock.

Santiago (1983) assigned a Middle to Late Miocene age for this lithologic unit. Correlation with the stratigraphic column of Pubellier and others (1991) yielded the same age range.
