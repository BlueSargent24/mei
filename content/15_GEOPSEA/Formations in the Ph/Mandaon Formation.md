  

The Mandaon Formation of MMAJ-JICA (1990) may be considered equivalent to the Kaal Formation. It consists of a thick sequence of dark, well-indurated volcanic sandstone and conglomerate, fragmental flows, volcanic rocks, and occasional parallel-bedded red calcarenites and manganese beds that is unconformably (?) overlain by the Late Oligocene-Early Miocene Sambulawan Formation of UNDP (1984) at Mandaon, Masbate Island. This formation is in thrust contact (underthrust) with the older Manapao Basalt and Calumpang Formation in the southwestern leg of the island. The Mandaon Formation is in a fork-shaped, NE-trending position at Balud-Mandaon, as a U-shaped body at Aroroy, and in peripheral position in Milagros. It is intruded by the Aroroy Quartz Diorite which gave a radiometric dating of 38 Ma (Middle-Late Eocene). The formation is therefore assigned an Eocene age, probably Early – Middle Eocene_.
