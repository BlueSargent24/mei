---

Lithology: Massive reef limestone

Stratigraphic_relations: Overlies the Loreto Formation

Distribution: Siargao Island, Surigao del Norte

Age: Pleistocene

Previous_Name: Siargao Formation (Sunga and Palaganas, 1986)

Renamed_by: MGB (2004)

---

 

Resting above the Loreto Formation at Siargao Island is the the Siargao Limestone consisting of massive, oolitic, porous reefal limestone. The limestone frequently contains masses of broken corals, shells and casts of small gastropods. Conspicuous bedding is present, particularly towards its base where it becomes increasingly sandy and marly. Well-bedded calcarenites, calcisiltites, and gritty sandstones characterize the base of the limestone. Sunga and Palaganas (1986) cites a report of Malicdem and others (1958) describing thinly bedded limestone containing Miocene to Pleistocene fossils that overlies an earlier Miocene limestone formation, probably the Timamana Limestone. The fossil assemblage indicates a Pleistocene age for the later Siargao Limestone..
