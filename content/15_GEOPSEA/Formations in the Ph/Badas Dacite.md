---

Lithology: Dacite

Stratigraphic_relations: Intrudes older deposits

Distribution: Barangay Bad-as and Placer area, Surigao del Norte; Masapelid Island

Age: Late Pliocene (Piacenzian)

Named_by: Santos and others (1962)

---

 

The Bad-as Dacite (Santos and others, 1962) occurs in limited outcrops near Barangay Bad-as and Placer area, Surigao del Norte. It consists of phenocrysts of quartz, biotite and plagioclase in a pale gray groundmass. It differs from the Ipil Andesite in its quartz content and relatively larger plagioclase phenocrysts.
