

---

Lithology: Basalt, sandstone, mudstone, shale

Stratigraphic_relations: Unconformably overlies the Nilabsan Formation

Distribution: Barrio Kaburacanan, Talakag Timber logging road in Kilapagan area, Bukidnon

Age: Eocene – Early Oligocene

Named_by: Santiago (1983)

---

 

The term Kilapagan Formation was first used by Santiago (1983) for the rocks typically exposed in Barrio Kaburacanan and along the Talakag Timber, Inc. logging road within the Kilapagan area, Bukidnon. This formation consists of slightly metamorphosed basalt flows and clastic rocks consisting of sandstone, shale and mudstone of Eocene to Early Oligocene age (Santiago, 1983).
