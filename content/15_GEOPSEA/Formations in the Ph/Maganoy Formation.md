

---

Lithology: Limestone, conglomerate, sandstone

Stratigraphic_relations: Conformable over the Kiamba Formation

Distribution: Maganoy River, Akir-akir Mountain, south-central Maguindanao

Age: Late Oligocene – Early Miocene

Thickness: ~ 600 m

Named_by: Froehlich and Melendres (1960)

---

 

The Maganoy Formation was named by Froehlich and Melendres (1960) for the sedimentary sequence along Maganoy River, south-central Maguindanao. The formation rests unconformably over the volcanic basement, probably corresponding to the Kiamba Formation. The Maganoy consists of dark gray to black fossiliferous limestone, pebble and cobble conglomerates, and greenish gray pebbly and orbitoidal sandstones. The formation underlies the Akir-Akir Mountain west of Cotabato Valley. Fossils indicate a Late Oligocene to Early Miocene age. Its estimated thickness is around 600 m (BMG, 1981).
