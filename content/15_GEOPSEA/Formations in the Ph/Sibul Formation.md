  

The Sibul Formation is considered part of the Madlum Formation in Bulacan. Corby and others (1951) divides the formation into an upper member consisting of volcanic flows and clastic rocks and a lower limestone member. As described by Corby and others (1951) it is well-exposed along Madlum River, east of the Sibul Springs_. (see [Madlum Formation](Madlum%20Formation.md)_).
