---

Lithology: Basalt, andesite, dacite, agglomerate

Stratigraphic_relations: Not reported

Distribution: Binaybay River, near San Teodoro poblacion, Mindoro Island

Age: Late Miocene to Early Pliocene

Previous_name: San Teodoro Volcanics (Miranda, 1980)

Renamed_by: MGB (2004)

---

 

This volcanic complex was previously named by Miranda (1980) as San Teodoro Volcanics for the exposures of volcanic rocks along Binaybay River, near the poblacion of San Teodoro. The formation consists of basalt flows with minor andesitic and dacitic phases and volcanic agglomerate. Ellipsoidal or pillow structures in the basalt flows indicate submarine conditions during deposition. These volcanic rocks were probably extruded during Late Miocene to Early Pliocene.
