---

Lithology: quartz schist, greenschist

Stratigraphic_correlation: Basement rocks

Distribution: San Fernando to Danao Point, Ticao Island

Age: Cretaceous?

Named_by: MGB (2004)

---

 

BMG (1981) mentioned the presence of basement rocks composed essentially of schists west of San Fernando to Danao Point on the southwestern coast of the island. At Talisay Point, the basement includes quartzites, argillites, and marbles. By virtue of their field occurrence and mineral assemblages similar to those of the Aroroy Schist in mainland Masbate, Aurelio (1992) implied a possible Cretaceous age for the basement schist in Ticao Island.
