 

The Virac Granodiorite is one of the intermediate plutonic rocks named after specific localities in the Luzon Central Cordillera. The Granodiorite was named by Schafer (1956) after exposures in Virac, Benguet. It could represent a facies of the Central Cordillera Diorite Complex.
