The oldest sedimentary sequence covering the Northern Sierra Madre was named Santa Fe Formation by MMAJ-JICA (1977). This outcrops mainly in the eastern part of Natbang and along the road from Santa Fe to Dalton Pass in Nueva Vizcaya. MMAJ-JICA (1975) recognizes a lower limestone member and an upper clastic member. The limestone is white to pinkish gray and is about 100 m thick (Ringenbach, 1992). Three local exposures of the limestone member have been observed to lie unconformably over the plutonic rocks of the Dupax Diorite Complex and the basaltic clastic rocks of the Caraballo Formation (Billedo, 1994). Large foraminifers in limestone samples from Dalton Pass indicate an age of Late Oligocene to Early Miocene. The total thickness of this formation is estimated at 800 m (JICA- MMAJ, 1975).
