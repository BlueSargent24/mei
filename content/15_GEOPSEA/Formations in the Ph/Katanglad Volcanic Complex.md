

---

Lithology: Basalt, andesite, dacite, pyroclastic rocks

Distribution: Bukidnon

Age: Pleistocene

Named_by: MGB (2004)

---

 

Mts. Katanglad and Kalatungan to its south are the more notable of a cluster of volcanic edifices in northern Bukidnon area. Other volcanic centers in the area include Pudung, Kilakron, Nanluyaw and Kidonging. Adventive domes of andesite and dacite are also present. Radiometric K-Ar dating of three basalt samples gave ages of 0.40 Ma, 0.27Ma and 0.25 Ma. On the other hand, a sample of shoshonitic basalt gave a K-Ar age of 0.52 Ma, while a dacite sample from an adventive dome was dated < zero, that is to say, <100 Ka (Sajona and others, 1997).
