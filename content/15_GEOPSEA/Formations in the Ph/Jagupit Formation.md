  

The Jagupit Formation was named by UNDP (1984) for the narrow-trending belt of clastic rocks east of the Mainit Valley and south of Puyo River at Agusan del Norte. The Jagupit consists of conglomerates, wackes and mudstones. It is equivalent to the Tugunan Formation at Surigao del Norte. (_see [Tugunan Formation](Tugunan%20Formation.md)_)
