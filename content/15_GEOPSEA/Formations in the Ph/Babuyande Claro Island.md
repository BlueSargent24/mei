  

Babuyan Claro island is part of the Batanes Group of Islands. The five volcanic centers of Babuyan Claro, namely Cayonan, Naydi, Dionisio, Mt. Pangasun, and Mt. Babuyan, consist mainly of a succession of calc-alkaline andesitic and basaltic andesitic lava flows. (_see [Babuyan Subprovince](Babuyan%20Subprovince.md)_)
