Overlying the Diliman Tuff is a sequence of unconsolidated fluvial, deltaic and marine deposits to which Purser and Diomampo (1995) proposed the name Manila Formation. This sequence is believed to have been laid down during Holocene time. Subsurface data from core drilling along the Light Rail Transit 2 (LRT 2) route from Santolan, Pasig to Recto, Manila indicate a thickness of about 800 m. The unconsolidated deposits consist of clay, silt, gravelly sand and tuffaceous silt.
