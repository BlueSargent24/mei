  

The Felsic Volcanic Rocks of Garcia and Mercado (1981) in Samar refers to a thick series of interlayered dacitic lavas, volcanic breccia and lapilli tuffs which is considered part of the Lawaan Formation. The Lawaan is thought to have been emplaced during the Paleogene (MMAJ-JICA, 1988), probably during Late-Cretaceous – Early Eocene. (_see [Lawaan Formation](Lawaan%20Formation.md)_)
