  

The Loquilocon Limestone of Garcia and Mercado (1981) is considered part of the Daram Formation in Samar Island. The Loquilocon Limestone is also equivalent to the Oligocene Malajog Limestone, which is sporadically distributed in western Samar (BED, 1986b). (_see [Daram Formation](Daram%20Formation.md)_)
