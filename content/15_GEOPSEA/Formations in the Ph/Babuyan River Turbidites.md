  

The Babuyan River Turbidites was named by UNDP (1985) for the sequence of Cretaceous-Eocene mudstones and sandstones along Babuyan River in Central Palawan. It is considered equivalent to the Boayan Formation. Suzuki and others (2001) considers the Babuyan River Turbidites as a facies of the Concepcion Phyllite.
