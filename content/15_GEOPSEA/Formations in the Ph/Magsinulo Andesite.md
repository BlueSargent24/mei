---

Lithology: Andesite

Stratigraphic_relations: Unconformably overlain by the Amlan Conglomerate

Distribution: Magsinulo, southeastern Negros Oriental

Age: Early – Late Pliocene

Previous_name: Magsinulo Andesite Flow Breccia (Ayson, 1987)

Renamed_by: MGB (2004)

---

 

The Magsinulo Andesite was previously named by Ayson (1987) as Magsinulo Andesite Flow Breccia for the exposures in the southeastern part of Negros Island. As described by Ayson (1987), this formation consists of andesite flow breccia and blocky andesite flows. The breccia shows angular clasts of hornblende andesite in a yellowish vitric matrix with phenocrysts of feldspars and ferromagnesian minerals. Ayson (1987) assigns an age of Early-Late Pliocene to this unit.
