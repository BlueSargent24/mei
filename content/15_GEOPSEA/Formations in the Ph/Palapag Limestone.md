  

The Palapag Limestone is the equivalent limestone member of the Calicoan Formation. It is distributed mainly along the coastal areas in eastern Samar and to a lesser extent, in westernmost Samar (BED, 1986b). It consists of coralline rubbles, limestone breccias, biocalcarenites and coral-algal deposits. (_see [Calicoan Formation](Calicoan%20Formation.md)_)
