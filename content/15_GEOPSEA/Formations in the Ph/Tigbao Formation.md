  

Melendres and Barnes (1967) consider the Tigbao as one of the formations comprising the Malabago when they raised the latter to group rank. The Tigbao is composed mainly of tuffaceous mudstone with interbedded conglomerate and sandy limestone. In MGB (2004), the Tigbao is considered a member of the Malabago Formation.
