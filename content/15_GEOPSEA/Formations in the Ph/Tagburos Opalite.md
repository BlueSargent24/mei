---

Lithology: Opalite

Distribution: Tagburos and Bacungan area, Puerto Princesa City, Palawan

Age: Probably Pleistocene

Named_by: De los Santos (1959)

---

 

The Tagburos Opalite refers to the irregular masses of yellowish- to mocha brown siliceous rocks exposed as separate hills in Tagburos, and Bacungan areas, Puerto Princesa City. Such occurrence is believed to have been deposited by hot springs during Pleistocene time. The deposits consist of massive dark gray chalcedony and variegated opaline silica. The chalcedony usually crops out along the peak of ridges while the opalite is commonly found along slopes around the chalcedony peaks.
