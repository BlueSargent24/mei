The Samar Ophiolite in eastern Samar is part of the Samar-Surigao segment of the Eastern Bicol-Eastern Mindanao Ophiolite Belt (BMG, 1981, 1986). It embodies a nearly complete ophiolite sequence consisting of peridotite, dunite, gabbro, sheeted dykes and pillow basalts (Garcia and Mercado, 1981; MMAJ-JICA, 1990; Moon and others, 1991). The ophiolite is characterized by a series of north trending thrust slices constituting a repetitive sequence of ophiolite suite and characterized as Alpine-type intrusion (Franco and others, 1993).

The [Giporlos Ultramafic Complex](Giporlos%20Ultramafic%20Complex.md), sheeted dikes and Camcuevas Volcanics in southeastern Samar comprise the Samar Ophiolite. The [Anagasi Formation](Anagasi%20Formation.md) in central Samar is correlative to the sheeted dikes and the [Camcuevas Volcanic Complex](Camcuevas%20Volcanic%20Complex.md) which are both overlain by the Upper Cretaceous [Balo Formation](Balo%20Formation.md). The [Lawaan Formation](Lawaan%20Formation.md), which underlies the Anagasi Formation, is partly equivalent to the [Felsic Volcanic Rocks](Felsic%20Volcanic%20Rocks.md) Rocks of Garcia and Mercado (1981) and BMG (1981). The Lawaan and Anagasi formations could be part of the ophiolite suite.
