  

The Labog Limestone was named by Martin (1972) for the dark gray Eocene limestone at Labog area, southern Palawan. It overlies a sequence of sandstone and shale identical to the clastic rocks of the Panas Formation. The Labog corresponds to the Sumbiling Limestone of Casasola (1956).
