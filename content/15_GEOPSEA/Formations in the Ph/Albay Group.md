  

The Albay Group was named by Corby and others (1951) for the suite of calcareous and highly fossiliferous sedimentary units deposited over tilted beds of the Bicol Formation. The formations comprising the Albay Group are: [Talisay Limestone](Talisay%20Limestone.md), [Aliang Siltstone](Aliang%20Siltstone.md), Paulba Sandstone and [Malama Siltstone](Malama%20Siltstone.md).
