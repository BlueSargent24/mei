  

The Mount Bilhogan and Batunan Conglomerates is a member of the Buso and Altar Formation of Melendres and Comsti (1951). The conglomerates are well exposed at Mount Bilhogan, near Sigaboy, and Batunan, east of Mati, Davao Oriental. The Buso and Altar Formation is equivalent to the Sigaboy Formation of MGB (1992).
