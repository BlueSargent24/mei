---

Lithology: Limestone

Stratigraphic_relations: Unconformable over the Unisan Formation

Distribution: Panaon, Bondoc Peninsula, Quezon

Age: Late Oligocene – Early Miocene

Named_by: Antonio (1961)

---

 

The Panaon Limestone was named by Antonio (1961) for the exposures at Panaon, Quezon. The Limestone also outcrops along Plaridel Road, Unisan - Panaon Road, Tumagay and Malicboy. The formation consists of medium- to thickly bedded bioclastic limestone, which unconformably overlies the Unisan Formation. Fossil assemblage in the limestone include _Lepidocyclina (Eulepidina) formosa_, _Spiroclypeus margaritatus_, _Lepidocyclina_ (N_ephrolepidina) parva_, _Cycloclypeus sp_. and few _Miogypsina sp_., indicating Late Oligocene – Early Miocene age (Lubas and others, 1998).
