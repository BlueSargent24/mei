

---

Lithology: Hornblende andesite

Stratigraphic_relations: not reported

Distribution: eastern coast of Panay and Calagnaan Island.

Age: Pleistocene

Previous_Name: Odiongan Volcanics (MMAJ-JICA, 1992)

Renamed_by: MGB (2004)

---

 

The Odiongan Andesite was previously named by MMAJ-JICA (1992) as Odiongan Volcanics and is distributed along the hilly regions of the east coast and at Calagnaan Island. The Odiongan consists of pale-grayish brown porphyritic hornblende andesite. Certain andesites that were previously lumped with the Odiongan were determined by MMAJ-JICA (1993) as altered hematite-stained facies of the Sibala Formation.
