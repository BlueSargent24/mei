  

The Alegria Andesite Porphyry of UNDP (1984) occurs as plugs along the western margin of the eastern highlands, near Mainit Valley, Agusan del Norte. The andesite is strongly plagiophyric hornblende andesite with occasional quartz phenocrysts (UNDP, 1984). It is probably equivalent to the Mabuhay Andesite (_see [Mabuhay Andesite](Mabuhay%20Andesite.md)_).
