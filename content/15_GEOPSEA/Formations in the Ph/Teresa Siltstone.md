  

The Binangonan Limestone of Smith (1906) was renamed by BMG (1981) as the Binangonan Formation to include the Teresa Tuffaceous Silt of Corby and others (1951) which was called Teresa Siltstone by MGB (2004). The Teresa Siltstone and the limestone are treated in MGB (2004) as the lower and upper members, respectively, of the Binangonan Formation. The **_Teresa Siltstone_** is essentially a 350-m thick sequence of tuffaceous calcareous siltstones and marl deposited by turbidity currents in a shallow basin (Schoell and Fuentes, 1989; Schoell and Casareo, 1989). The overall sedimentological characteristics of the unit, as observed by Foronda and Schoell (1987), suggest that the unit represents shallow water proximal turbidites_.
