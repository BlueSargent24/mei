  

UNDP (1987) describes the Paco Andesite as an extinct volcano with a conical peak at 524 masl north of Bgy. Paco in Surigao del Norte. The cone is surrounded by gently dipping andesitic flows and lahars. It is considered coeval with the Maniayao Andesite. (_see [Paco Volcanics](Paco%20Volcanics.md) and [Maniayao Andesite](Maniayao%20Andesite.md)_).
