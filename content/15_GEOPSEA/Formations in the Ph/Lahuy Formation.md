

---

Lithology: Basalt, dacite, sandstone

Stratigraphic_relations: Not reported

Distribution: Lahuy Island and adjoining islands, off Caramoan Peninsula

Age: Middle – Late Miocene

Named_by: BMG (1981)

---

 

The intercalated sandstone, basalt and dacite flows reported by Miranda (1976) in the southwestern part of Lahuy Island and in some of the adjoining islands are called Lahuy Formation by BMG (1981). The sandstone is well bedded, light gray, fairly indurated, tuffaceous and rich in magnetite sand. The formation was assigned an age of Middle to Late Miocene by BMG (1981).
