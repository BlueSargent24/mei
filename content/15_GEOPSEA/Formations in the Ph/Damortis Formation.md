---

Lithology: Sandstone, calcarenite, siltstone, limestone and marl

Stratigraphic_relations: Unconformable over the Cataguintingan Formation

Distribution: Damortis beach, Pangasinan

Age: Pleistocene

Thickness: 50-200 m

Named_by: Corby and others (1951)

---

 

Along Damortis beach near the PNR railway station is a small exposure of the Damortis Formation consisting of westward dipping sandstones, calcarenites, siltstones and marl. It rests unconformably over the Cataguintingan Formation near the type locality. This gently dipping sequence of clastic rocks commonly contains molluscan fossils. In places the sandstones are dark colored due to the presence of heavy minerals. To the north, resting on the Amlang Formation at Bacnotan, is the 20-m thick **[Bacnotan Limestone](Bacnotan%20Limestone.md)**, regarded by Maleterre (1989) as a facies of the Damortis Formation. Tamesis and others (1981) estimate the thickness of the formation to range from 50m to 200m on the basis of seismic data. Javelosa (1994) reports a 14C dating of 28,250 ± 345 years BP at the top of a sandstone horizon in raised tidal flats along the Damortis coast. The formation is considered Pleistocene in age.
