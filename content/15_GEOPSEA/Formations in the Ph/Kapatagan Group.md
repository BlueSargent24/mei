  

The Kapatagan Group was named by Tupas (1952) for the Pleistocene sequence of tuffs, sandstones and conglomerates in the Kapatagan lowlands, western Misamis Oriental. Clasts of the conglomerate are andesitic, most of them pumiceous. The Kapatagan is equivalent to the Bukidnon Formation. (_see [Bukidnon Formation](Bukidnon%20Formation.md)_)
