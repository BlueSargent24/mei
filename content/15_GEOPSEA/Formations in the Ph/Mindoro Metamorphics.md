  

The Mindoro Metamorphics was named by Teves (1953) for the metamorphic rocks in Bongabon area in southern Mindoro. The metamorphic complex includes greenschists, phyllites, slates, gneisses, amphibolites and marble. It was renamed by MMAJ-JICA (1984) as Halcon Metamorphics with type locality around Mt. Halcon.
