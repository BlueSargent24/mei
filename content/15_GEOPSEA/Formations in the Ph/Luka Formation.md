

---

Lithology: Sandstone, conglomerate and mudstone with limestone lenses

Stratigraphic_relations: Unconformable over the Malubog Formation

Distribution: Luka area northeast of Balamban, Cebu Island

Age: Middle Miocene.

Named_by: Santos-Yñigo (1951)

---

 

The Luka Formation was introduced by Santos-Yñigo (1951) for the alternating beds of sandstone, conglomerate and mudstone with limestone lenses exposed in the west coast, about 15 km east-northeast of Balamban, Cebu Island. It is unconformable over the lower Malubog Formation. The age of the formation is Middle Miocene.
