  

This group name was introduced by Hashimoto and Sato (1973) to include the [Bacuit](Bacuit%20Formation.md), [Minilog](Minilog%20Limestone.md), [Liminangcong](Liminangcong%20Formation.md) and [Guinlo](Guinlo%20Formation.md) formations, exposed in the Malampaya Sound area in northern Palawan. The Malampaya Sound Group also embraces the Coron Formation of Wolfart and others (1986).
