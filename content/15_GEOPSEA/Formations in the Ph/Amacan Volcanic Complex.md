The Amacan Volcanic Complex refers to the Holocene volcanic deposits in the vicinity of Lake Leonard in Davao, where the Amacan Mine of North Davao Mining Corporation is located. Aside from Maniayao Volcano in Surigao, it is the only area in eastern Mindanao affected by Pleistocene-Holocene volcanism. This volcanic activity is manifested as domes, plugs, flows and pyroclastic rocks of andesitic to dacitic composition. A thin pyroclastic blanket of breccias, lapilli tuffs and ash tuffs drapes an irregular erosional surface around the lake area. A sample of carbonized wood from the volcanic ash around the Lake Leonard area gave a 14C date of 1800 years (PNOC-EDC unpublished internal report, 1983).
