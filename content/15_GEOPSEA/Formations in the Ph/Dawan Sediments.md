  

The Dawan sediments was named by Melendres and Comsti (1951) for the sequence of chert, jasper and ferruginous shale with lenticular interbeds of limestone at Dawan, northwest of Pujada Bay, Davao Oriental. It is equivalent to the Iba Formation that represents the pelagic sedimentary cover of the Pujada Ophiolite. (_see [Iba Formation](Iba%20Formation.md)_)
