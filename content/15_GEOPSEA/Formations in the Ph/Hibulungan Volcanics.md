  

The Hibulungan Volcanics in western Leyte (White Eagle Overseas Oil Co., 1957, in BMG, 1981) is apparently coeval with the the Kanturao Volcanics in the central highlands of Leyte. The Hibulungan is reported to unconformably overlie the lower Taog Formation. (_see [Kanturao Volcanic Complex](Kanturao%20Volcanic%20Complex.md)_)
