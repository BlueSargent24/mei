  

The Dingalan Formation was named by Rutland (1968) for the Late Oligocene sequence of coarse epiclastic breccias, fine greywacke and chert mudstones that are typically exposed along the Dingalan Forest Products Co. road in the Laur-Dingalan fault zone in Nueva Ecija. The Dingalan is equivalent to the Mamparang Formation. (_see [Mamparang Formation](Mamparang%20Formation.md)_)
