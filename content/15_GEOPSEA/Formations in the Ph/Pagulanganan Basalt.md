  

The Pangulanganan Basalt of UNDP (1984) at Agusan del Norte could be part of the Dinagat Ophiolite that has been dismembered. It consists of pillow basalts and minor basalt breccia found along Cabadbaran River and its tributary, Pagulanganan Creek. (_see [Dinagat Ophiolite](Dinagat%20Ophiolite.md)_)
