 

The Tumalo is a member of the Caguray Formation in Mindoro. Calcareous mudstones, siltstones and grainstones comprise the Tumalo Member, exposed along the Caguray and Tumalo rivers. Calcareous nannofossils and planktonic foraminifera indicate an Early Oligocene age for this member (Sarewitz and Karig, 1986_). (see [Caguray Formation](Caguray%20Formation.md)_)
