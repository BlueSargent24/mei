The Linut-od is another coal-bearing formation in southern Cebu which was found conformably overlying and intertonguing with the Butong Limestone. It was named by Barnes and others (1958) for the shale, sandstone and conglomerate with occasional coal beds exposed at Barrio Linut-od, Argao. It is almost lithologically similar to the Calagasan except that in this formation the shales and mudstones are more dominant. The coal beds of the formation are mostly located in the lower sections of the unit. Its thickest exposure reaches more than 1,300 m along Maangtud Creek. The age of Linut-od is Early Miocene.
