  

The Maibu Mudstone and Sandstone, together with the [Dimuluk Conglomerate](Dimuluk%20Conglomerate.md) in the southern part of Cotabato Valley area, comprises the equivalent sequence in the south of the Late Miocene to Early Pliocene Nicaan Formation in northern Cotabato Valley area. (_see [Nicaan Formation](Nicaan%20Formation.md)_)
