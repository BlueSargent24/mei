  

The Santa Cruz Sediments was named by Cruz and Lingat (1966) for the exposures of Pliocene – Pleistocene clastic rocks on the western side of Buruanga Peninsula in Panay Island. It was renamed Libertad Formation by MGB (2004) and is considered equivalent to the [Apdo Formation](Apdo%20Formation.md) of Momongan (1979) in the Antique Range. (_see [Libertad Formation](Libertad%20Formation.md)_).
