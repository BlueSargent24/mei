Basement rocks: [[Angat Ophiolite]]

Going to Wawa Dam, it's being quarried
- highly fractured since near EVF

On top is the Kinabuan Formation
- microfossils, based on forams (lower than cretaceous)
- does not occur as an individual unit
- occurs as clasts of the Maybangain formation -> supported by Doc Q

Maybangain
- interbedded sandstone
- Eocene
- Mansungi limestone, sinkholes, etc
	- in 2010, umakyat sila, bedded siya. Question: is it part of maybangain or independent?
	- masungi limestone member age early to late paleocene

Binangonan Limestone
- reefal limestone
- upper oligo to lower miocene

Angat limestone
- wawa dam

Madlum formation
- Conglomerates, interbedded mudstones and sandstones
- upper lower to lower middle miocene
- 3 facies
	- bottom to top: Alagao Volcanics, Buenacop Limestone, clastic facies
